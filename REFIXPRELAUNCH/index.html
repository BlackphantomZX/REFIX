People replace smartphones over small, repairable issues.

A new phone often costs far more than a repair, leaving the old device unused or discarded.

How it's solved today

Local repair shops, brand-authorized service centres, informal technicians, or replacing the phone altogether.

Why that falls short

Uncertainty around repair quality, pricing, genuine parts and reliability — the gap Right to Repair India is pushing to close.

Why now

Global smartphone replacement cycles have stretched to nearly four years, and second-life devices are growing fast (Counterpoint Research).
